    Build the vocabulary

    We read every word of every sentence, one by one. The first time we meet a word it gets the next free ID (0, 1, 2 …). If we already know the word, we skip it. The result is the vocabulary — a list of all V distinct words.

    Limitations of one-hot encoding

    One-hot vectors are simple and lossless, but they say nothing about meaning, and they get huge as the vocabulary grows.

    1 · Every pair of words is equally different

    What comes next

    One-hot gives a vector for each word. To represent a whole sentence or document with a single vector, we can add the one-hot vectors of its words together — that is Bag of Words. Later, Word2Vec replaces one-hot vectors with short, dense vectors that do capture meaning.
