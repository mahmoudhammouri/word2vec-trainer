    Term frequency (TF)

    How important is a word inside one document? TF counts how often the word appears, divided by the document length — so long and short documents can be compared fairly.

    TF(word, doc) = (times the word appears in the doc) ÷ (number of words in the doc)

    Inverse document frequency (IDF)

    How special is a word across all documents? First count in how many documents the word appears (document frequency, DF). Words found everywhere get a low IDF; rare words get a high IDF. Click a word to see which documents contain it.

    TF × IDF

    Multiply the two: a word gets a high score when it is frequent in this document (high TF) and rare in the others (high IDF). The top-scoring words are the document's keywords.

    TF-IDF(word, doc) = TF(word, doc) × IDF(word)

    Still missing: meaning

    TF-IDF still treats every word as a separate column: cat and dog, or film and movie, are as unrelated as cat and goal. It also ignores word order. Word2Vec learns dense vectors where similar words end up close together.
