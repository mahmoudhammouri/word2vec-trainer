The documents

Bag of Words turns a whole document (a sentence, a review, an email…) into one vector of word counts. Here every line is one document. Edit them or load an example set.

Documents

    Pre-processing

    1. Lower-case everything and drop punctuation.
    2. Split into words (tokens).
    3. Optionally remove stop words — shown crossed out.
    2

    Vocabulary = the columns

    Bag of Words first collects every distinct word in all documents. Each vocabulary word becomes one column (one position) of the document vectors. Every document will be described by the same V columns.

    Vocabulary

    The idea: throw the words into a bag

    3

    Count the words of one document

    Start with a vector of V zeros. Read the document word by word and add +1 to that word's column. The final vector is the document's Bag-of-Words vector.

    Document

    Bag-of-Words vector

    Same as adding up the one-hot vectors of all its words.
    4

    The document-term matrix

    Do the same for every document and stack the vectors: one row per document, one column per word. This table is the input for classic machine-learning models (spam filters, sentiment, search…). Click a row or column to highlight it.

    Document-term matrix
